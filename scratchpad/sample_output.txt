== Result 3: held, deeper -- survivable harm gets more draws; an IRREVERSIBLE tail gets one ==
No.14: over a lifetime the probe eventually samples a SURVIVABLE rare event (latent).
No.15: an un-survivable tail event has no second draw -- the first firing is terminal, so
the binding coverage is the PRE-event catch prob and the miss (1-phi_s) is catastrophic.
only a probe with power over the WHOLE stream holds -- every decisive act pre-screened --
